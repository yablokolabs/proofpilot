ProofPilot AI

Don't Just Trust AI. Verify It. The credibility check before you hit publish.

Look confident. Stay credible.

User, core loop, proof

The kernel

The question
"Will this AI-generated content embarrass me when someone checks the facts?"
The signature moment
A confident paragraph: one claim holds, one cannot be checked, and one impressive statistic turns red. A click shows the evidence that contradicts it.
Who
Alex, a marketing professional who publishes AI-assisted posts, presentations, and client reports, and checks facts by hand in Google today.

Boundary

P0: must work end to end
  • Text input
  • Real claim extraction
  • Real evidence retrieval
  • Supported / Contradicted / Unverified classification
  • Source citations
  • Inline claim highlighting on the original text
  • Publication readiness summary: "REVIEW BEFORE PUBLISHING" or "ALL CHECKED CLAIMS SUPPORTED"
  • A working end-to-end demo
P1
  • Clickable evidence cards
  • Professional, responsive UI
Integrity rules
  • No fabricated sources, URLs, citations, or results
  • A source must be relevant to the claim
  • Contradicting evidence is not the same as insufficient evidence
  • Missing or weak evidence gives Unverified
  • LLM confidence is not proof
  • No claim of full coverage if statements were skipped
  • No "safe", "guaranteed true", or "verified" wording
  • Clear loading, error, and empty states
Explicitly cut
  • Browser extensions
  • PDF uploads
  • User accounts and authentication
  • Multi-agent orchestration
  • Background monitoring
  • Social media integrations
  • Automated publishing
  • Complex dashboards
  • Billing
  • Enterprise features
