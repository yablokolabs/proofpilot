ProofPilot AI: Product Requirements

One screen. One loop. Each verdict can be traced to a real source.

Screen layout in the report state

The core journey, step by step

Arrive. A text box, the input-length limit, and the Check claims button. No sample result before a real check.
  • Empty input cannot be submitted.
  • Text above the limit is blocked with a message. No silent truncation.
Paste and check. Alex pastes an AI-generated paragraph and selects Check claims.
  • Claims are statistics, historical facts, research findings, and similar statements.
  • Each claim maps to an exact span of the original text.
Wait. A clear indication that the analysis is in progress.
  • A second check cannot start at the same time.
  • On failure: a clear error, the text stays in the box, and no partial or invented report appears.
Report. The summary, then the original text with highlights.
  • REVIEW BEFORE PUBLISHING if a claim is contradicted or unverified.
  • ALL CHECKED CLAIMS SUPPORTED if all checked claims are supported.
  • No checkable claims: a message, and neither status.
  • Partial check: the checked count and a warning.
Inspect. A click on a highlight opens its evidence card.
  • Claim, status, short explanation.
  • Citations with title, URL, and excerpt, all from retrieved sources.
  • Unverified cards give the reason.

The three statuses

StatusMeaning
SupportedThe retrieved evidence agrees with the claim.
ContradictedThe retrieved evidence disagrees with the claim.
UnverifiedThe evidence is missing, weak, conflicting, ambiguous, inaccessible, or not relevant.

Decisions and open items

Sources
  • Primary sources first: official statistics, research papers, an organisation's own documentation.
  • Reputable secondary reporting when primary evidence is not available.
  • Each citation: title, URL, relevant excerpt.
  • No source, URL, or excerpt that was not retrieved.
Look and feel
  • Clean editorial look, readable typography, restrained colours.
  • Text labels for status. Never colour alone.
  • No chatbot-style UI.
Deferred
  • Saved reports or history
  • Sharing or export
  • Rewrite suggestions
  • Very long documents
Open questions (agent assumptions)
  • Values of the input limit and the claim limit: for the spec.
  • Evidence card beside on wide, below on narrow.
  • No persistence.
  • No one-click sample paragraph.
  • No exclusion list for sources.
